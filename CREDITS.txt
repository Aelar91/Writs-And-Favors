Writs and Favors: credits and data sources
==========================================

The addon's code is its own. It bundles no libraries. Its data (Data.lua) is made from these sources:

Polestar (https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar), GPL-3.0
  The travel data: each side's flight points and flight times, the zeppelins, boats and the tram, where the hand-in NPCs and
  the mailboxes are, and the riding times between them (worked out on Polestar's navigation grid). Polestar's own
  sources for these are in its THIRD_PARTY.md: Blizzard's client data tables by way of wago.tools, pfQuest (MIT,
  Copyright (c) 2017-2021 Eric Mauser (Shagu)), QuestieDB, CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0) and Carbonite's zone
  crossings (GPL-3.0).

Wowhead (https://www.wowhead.com/forever)
  Facts looked up there: which item each Craftsman's Writ asks for and how many, its reputation and coin pouch, who
  takes it, and what each Waylaid Crate holds and gives.

wago.tools (https://wago.tools)
  Blizzard's client data tables: flight prices (TaxiPath) and the items' names, stack sizes and qualities.

World of Warcraft and its game data are (c) Blizzard Entertainment. This addon is not made or endorsed by Blizzard.

Works with, when they are installed (none of them is included or needed):
  RestedXP Guides (the route is sent to it as a guide), OpenMap (the route is drawn on its map), Auctionator and
  TradeSkillMaster (their last prices, before your own scan), LibSharedMedia and EllesmereUI (their Expressway font,
  if you have it).
